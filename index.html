💾 Data Backup & Restore

Your CRM data is saved in this browser. Export a backup regularly so you never lose your students, sessions, or packages - even if your browser resets.

📄
Export for GitHub
Downloads a complete index.html file with all your data baked in. Upload straight to GitHub to update your live CRM.
⬇️
Export Data Backup
Downloads your data as a JSON file. Save it to Google Drive or email it to yourself as a safety copy.
⬆️
Restore from Backup
Upload a previously exported JSON backup to restore all your data. This will replace your current data.
⚠️ Important
Clearing your browser data, switching browsers, or using a different device will NOT have access to your data. Always keep a recent backup saved externally (Google Drive recommended).
